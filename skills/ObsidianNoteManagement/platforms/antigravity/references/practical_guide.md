# Practical Guide to Using Obsidian

## Installation
1. **Download Obsidian**: Visit the official Obsidian website and download the installer for your platform.
2. **Install Obsidian**: Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

## Creating a New Vault
1. **Launch Obsidian**: Open the application after installation.
2. **Create New Vault**: Click on 'Create New Vault', name it, and choose a location on your computer or cloud storage.

## Creating and Organizing Notes
1. **Create a New Note**: Click on the new note icon in the navigation pane.
2. **Add Headings**: Use two pound signs followed by a space to create headings.
3. **Add Checklists**: Use a dash followed by a space and square brackets to create checklists.

## Linking Notes
1. **Link Related Notes**: Use double square brackets to link related notes. For example, `[[Double Chocolate Chip Cookie]]`.

## Integrating AI Tools
1. **Connect Claude**: Point Claude to your Obsidian vault location to allow it to access your notes.
2. **Ask Questions**: Use Claude to search your vault and create new notes.
