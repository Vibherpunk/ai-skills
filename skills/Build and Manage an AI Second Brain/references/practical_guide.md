# Practical Guide to Building an AI Second Brain

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for setting up and using an AI Second Brain.

## Step 1: Connect Your Apps
1. Navigate to the AI Second Brain platform.
2. Click on "Feed Brain" and connect apps like Gmail, Outlook, Notion, and Microsoft Teams.

## Step 2: Upload or Create Content
1. Upload existing files or create new documents directly within the platform.
2. Ensure all relevant data is included to maximize the AI's effectiveness.

## Step 3: Record and Sync Meetings
1. Use an AI recording device (e.g., SecondBrain Note) to capture in-person conversations.
2. The recordings are automatically transcribed and integrated into your Second Brain.

## Step 4: Query Your Second Brain
1. Ask questions like "What do I need to know before my meeting with Elizabeth?"
2. The AI retrieves relevant information from emails, documents, and meeting notes.

## Step 5: Automate Tasks
1. Use AI to draft emails, create project plans, and generate presentations.
2. Example prompts: "Summarize today's meeting and draft a follow-up email with next steps."
