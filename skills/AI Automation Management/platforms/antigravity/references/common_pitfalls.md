## Common Pitfalls in AI Automation

### Vague Instructions

**Issue**: Ambiguous prompts lead to irrelevant results.

**Example**: "Analyze my channel" vs. "Analyze my channel's Q2 performance focusing on viewer retention."

**Solution**: Always specify the goal, constraints, and desired format.

### Over-reliance on AI

**Issue**: Blindly accepting AI outputs without validation.

**Example**: Using an AI-generated Excel sheet without verifying data accuracy.

**Solution**: Regularly review and refine AI outputs to ensure reliability.

### Data Security

**Issue**: Granting AI access to sensitive data without proper controls.

**Example**: Allowing Claude Code to access confidential emails without approval.

**Solution**: Implement strict permissions and monitor AI actions.
