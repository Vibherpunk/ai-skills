# Core Concepts of Obsidian

## Vault
A vault in Obsidian is a folder that stores all your notes, attachments, and settings. It acts as a container for your knowledge base, allowing you to organize and manage your notes efficiently. You can create multiple vaults for different projects or areas of interest.

## Markdown
Markdown is a lightweight markup language used in Obsidian for formatting text. It allows you to create headings, lists, links, and more with simple syntax. For example, to create a heading, you use two pound signs followed by a space and the heading text.

## Graph View
The graph view in Obsidian provides a visual representation of how your notes are connected. As you link notes together, the graph view updates to show these connections, helping you see the relationships between different pieces of information.
